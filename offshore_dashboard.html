Testing Resilience Aspects of Operation Options for Offshore Wind Farms beyond the End-of-Life

Interactive reproduction of the educational notebook offshore_python_uebung_full_version.ipynb — wave-driven fatigue, structural degradation and network resilience for the three end-of-life strategies.

Citation: Köpke, C.; Mielniczek, J.; Stolz, A. Testing Resilience Aspects of Operation Options for Offshore Wind Farms beyond the End-of-Life. Energies 2023, 16, 4771. https://doi.org/10.3390/en16124771 · Code reworked for educational purposes by Tobias Rinnert.

Every figure below is recomputed live from the adjustable parameters shown with each chapter. Drag a slider and watch the whole downstream chain update. Legends sit to the side and scroll, so every trace label stays visible. All randomness is seeded for reproducibility.

1

Functions

Four building blocks are defined before any data is generated. They implement the equations from the paper (Rayleigh wave statistics and Eurocode 3 fatigue) and the repair rule.

Eq. 1
f(x; σ) = (x / σ²) · e^( −x² / 2σ² )
Rayleigh distribution (significant wave height → frequency). Multiplied by the monthly load-change count to estimate how many times a given height x hits the structure.
Eq. 2
Hmax = Hsig + | plus | ,   plus = U( 0 , Hsig · (0.07·Δyear + 3) )
Maximum wave height for a month from its significant wave height and the years since the base year, capped at maxH.
Eq. 3
Hfail(N) = max( −½·ln(N) + Hrel , Hmin )
Fatigue limit (DIN EN 1993-1-9 / Eurocode 3): the wave height a structure can still endure for a given number of load changes N.
Repair
broken: [ 0 , 0 , 1 ]
After a unit breaks it stays offline for 2 months, then works again for at least one month before it can break again (non-overlapping repair windows).

4

Calculate the cumulative sum of load changes over time

Load changes accumulate per discrete wave height. When a height exceeds what Eq. 3 allows for that accumulated load, the failure probability for that month jumps to phigh (else plow). The left figure keeps the structure in continuous use; the right figure decommissions it (load accumulation resets to zero) and rebuilds.

5

Create an offshore substation (OSS) network, destroy something and plot the performance

A flat network of numOSS · numTurbinesPerOSS + numOSS units is built. In months with waves larger than the damage threshold each unit breaks with probability prop/propDe, then follows the repair rule. Status is the fraction of working units; energy is their total MW output, ramped according to the chosen mode.

Parameters — network & damage
Notebook filters months with Hmax > 15 m before applying breakage.
Status over time

Energy output [MW]
Total operational power, mode-dependent. The area under the curve is the cumulative energy yield.
